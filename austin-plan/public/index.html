How does this affect my plan?

High soreness, fatigue or pain can reduce or pause upcoming running. Lower scores can help support a small mileage restoration, which still needs your approval. Skipping a check-in does not prevent viewing or following the plan, but the app may keep progression more conservative. Hosted changes apply at the next hourly review.
